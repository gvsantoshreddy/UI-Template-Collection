# AVANT VÉLOUR — Luxury Atelier & Ready-to-Wear Flagship

AVANT VÉLOUR is a high-end luxury fashion designer brand and digital flagship store featuring editorial women's eveningwear, couture bridal gowns, and bespoke men's tailoring.

## Key Features

1. **Dual Gender Flagship (Women's & Men's)**:
   - **Women's Collection**: 36+ luxury dresses across Evening Gowns, Cocktail Dresses, Red Carpet, Bridal & Couture, Silk Satin, Lace, and Floral Maxis.
   - **Men's Collection**: Sartorial double-breasted suits, cashmere trench coats, silk velvet bandhgalas, embroidered sherwanis, kurtas, watches, and fine leather goods.

2. **Advanced Product Discovery & Filtering**:
   - Comprehensive category mega-menus.
   - Price range, size, shade/color filters.
   - Sorting by Newest, Price (Low-High & High-Low), and Best Selling.

3. **Immersive Product Detail Pages**:
   - **Hover-to-Zoom Fabric Inspection**: Smooth high-resolution magnification on mouse hover to inspect textile weaves, silk textures, and tailoring stitches.
   - Multiple image galleries and related masterworks.

4. **Atelier Innovations**:
   - **Virtual Try-On Simulator**: Interactive avatar fitting preview for garments.
   - **AI Atelier Concierge**: Powered by Google Gemini AI API to provide executive styling and occasion advice.
   - Shopping bag slide-over, wishlist drawer, and secure checkout simulation.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide Icons, Motion.
- **Backend / API**: Node.js, Express, @google/genai SDK.
- **Build Tool**: Vite & Esbuild.
